Wrace Tower, Abuja

Between the federal institutions of the Three Arms Zone and the diplomatic streets of Maitama, on the avenue that carries the capital's business to its door, sits a fifteen-storey block of bronze and glass directly opposite World Trade Center Abuja.

Wrace Tower offers 13,550 m² of lettable offices over fourteen floors. Each office floor carries 1,000 m² of suites on a 1,200 m² plate: one anchor suite of exactly 500 m² and a handful of smaller suites from 100 m², every one on the window wall, with a pantry and breakout around the core.

The ground floor is an eight-metre arcade behind a colonnade: a lobby with four lifts, a pharmacy, a café on the plaza and an event hall. The fourteenth floor pairs a 550 m² full-floor office with a walled 450 m² wellness suite: gym, spa, sauna, steam and a 25-metre pool for every tenant. Parking is underground.

Location

Central Business District, Abuja

Diagrammatic, not to scale. Constitution Avenue sits inside the capital's principal government and diplomatic district: the National Assembly, the Federal Secretariat, the Central Bank and NNPC Towers are minutes away; the airport is about thirty minutes by road.

The
building

Fifteen storeys · 1,200 m² floor plate

A rectangular block, 40 by 30 metres and about 65 metres tall, wrapped in a dark bronze curtain wall of closely spaced vertical mullions and grey-bronze glass. The ground floor steps back behind a colonnade of dark steel columns; the roof carries a low plant screen. No setbacks, no curves.
